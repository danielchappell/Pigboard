# Work Item Intake

Use this when turning vague work into a queue item.

## Required Fields

- Objective
- Context links
- Scope
- Non-goals
- Acceptance criteria
- Verification required
- Worker type
- Initial status
- Priority
- Blockers or open decisions

## Discipline

Make assumptions loud. Do not hide uncertainty in polished prose. If the item
needs a product decision, say so and keep it out of `Ready`.

Good intake makes later workers boring: they should know what to do, what not to
do, and how their work will be judged.
