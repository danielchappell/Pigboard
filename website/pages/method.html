Set a useful goal. Understand the choices. See the evidence. Pigboard is being built to connect that whole journey for your team.

This is the intended product experience, based on the development method we use ourselves. Public hosted access is not available yet.

Keep the customer outcome in view

Start with what your customers need and how your team will judge success. Discovery should expose assumptions and constraints before they become expensive surprises.

The intended journey carries that context through planning, implementation and verification. Recommendations, decisions and delivery evidence stay connected to the goal, so your team can understand why the work is moving in a particular direction.

Your team receives a result to assess, with checks, review findings and remaining limitations. Acceptance and release remain decisions for the people with that authority.

Steer when priorities change

Project control means being able to change direction after work starts. The experience we're building should show what a new request changes, what can continue and which assumptions or checks need another look.

Illustrative example: CSV invoice export is underway when a customer asks for PDF. PDF adds document layout and new checks. The lead decides: “Finish CSV first. Keep PDF queued for the next priority decision.”

The intended result is a shared decision with a clear scope and reason. Teammates and agents can continue from the same context.

Your team sets goals, constraints, budgets and approval authority. Pigboard is being designed to coordinate work within those boundaries and bring consequential choices back to the right people. Completing an agent task does not authorize a scope expansion or release.

Confidence that survives the handoff

A useful project view should tell you what finished, what supports that conclusion and what is still uncertain. Checks and independent review help your team judge the result; they cannot guarantee correctness.

When work is interrupted, the goal is to preserve completed work, make the failure visible and show how to continue. The next person should not need to reconstruct a private conversation to take over.

That is the dependability standard Pigboard is being built to meet. Explore the current availability before planning to use the hosted product.
