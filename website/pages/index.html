Start with the customer outcome

Your team sets the goal and agrees what a useful result looks like.

Customers need their invoice history for accounting.
Let them export the current filtered view as CSV.

Change direction with context

CSV work is underway when a customer asks for PDF. The recommendation explains that PDF adds document layout and new checks; CSV can continue within the agreed scope.

You choose to finish CSV first and queue PDF. The decision stays with the project, so the team works from the same priorities.

Decide with evidence in front of you

The intended handoff shows the export to try, checks and review results, and remaining limitations. Your team can assess whether it meets the agreed need and decide what ships.
