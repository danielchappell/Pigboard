Pigboard is in development.

We're building AI software delivery for leads who want to direct the project, understand its progress and keep their team in control. Public hosted access is not open yet.

Current tools

The private repository contains experimental development tools: reusable agent roles and skills, a GitHub Projects runtime for local Codex work, a standalone Cursor command and an offline workflow preview.

These tools require authorized access to the private repository. They are not public downloads or the complete hosted experience described on this site.

The product we're building

A shared delivery journey from intent to acceptance, with visible progress, informed decisions and evidence your team can assess.

The intended hosted product brings the supported AI development lifecycle together around your project. That complete experience remains in development.

Public access

There is no public signup, trial or hosted workspace available today. This website introduces Pigboard and the experience we're building.
