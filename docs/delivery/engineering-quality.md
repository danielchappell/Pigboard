## Author assessment

Intuitive: focused methods and three sequential commands expose useful outcomes.
Discoverable: selection tables, complete generated examples and CLI help let people
and agents act. Delightful: local judgment and existing-test reuse avoid unnecessary
questions and rituals. Token saver: one plan and focused loading limit repeated
context; comparisons retain detail without a weighted headline. Secure: execution
authority is unchanged; records do not execute commands and operators own grading
and storage. Agents are equal: structured results and readable recipes serve both
audiences. Dependable: exact revisions, failed/missing attempts and separate
qualification prevent unsupported success; hashes do not authenticate grading.

## Remaining acceptance

Owner review precedes comparative model trials. Then prepare realistic fixtures,
the verified target-model host, independent graders and an agreed spend ceiling.
Keep each development/confirmation version's results. Bounded real-repository
acceptance and owner-authorized merge/release follow under existing product gates.
Neither skill publication nor this framework resolves the mixed worker bundle's
separate activation prerequisites.
